Government of India & Armed Forces Technical Internships

Authorized engineering positions, laboratory apprenticeships, and defense technology projects across the Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force, DRDO, ISRO, and Central PSUs.

Official Application & Clearance Protocol

01. College Bonafide NOC Submit an official endorsement letter signed by your College Principal or Dean of Academic Affairs confirming enrolled student status and academic standing.
02. Security Clearance & Police Verification Candidates selected for Indian Armed Forces formations and DRDO/ISRO labs must complete Annexure 'A' police verification and submit valid Indian citizenship credentials.
03. Gate Pass & Non-Disclosure (NDA) Strict adherence to Official Secrets Act provisions. Trainees are issued biometric RFID temporary passes; personal storage drives are restricted in classified areas.
